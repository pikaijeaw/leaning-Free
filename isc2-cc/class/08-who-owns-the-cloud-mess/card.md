# Lesson 8. Who Owns the Cloud Mess

**Listen:** who-owns-the-cloud-mess.mp3 (2 min)

**The idea, in three lines**
- **Shared responsibility:** the provider secures the physical infrastructure and the virtualization layer; the customer always owns its data, identities and access, and configuration.
- The middle moves with the model. **IaaS:** you do everything above the hardware, including patching the operating system. **PaaS:** the provider also runs the OS and tooling; you own your apps. **SaaS:** the provider runs almost everything; your settings and data stay yours.
- Transfer moves the bill, not the accountability: a cloud provider never takes over responsibility for your customers' data.

**Impact:** "who is responsible?" cloud questions stop being guesses, and you know which of your own cloud settings are yours to look after.

**Who says it:** 4 of the 5 pages on cloud teach shared responsibility this way; none disagree.

**Try it** · Quick Win · 10 min
Pick three cloud apps you use (for example email, photos, a shared drive). For each one, draw two columns:
> **1.** Provider handles: servers, buildings, the platform.
> **2.** Mine: my password and second factor, who I've shared with, and a copy of anything I can't lose.
> **3.** Check one thing in the "mine" column now: any file shared as "anyone with the link" that shouldn't be?

**Rule to keep**
In any cloud, my data and my logins are mine to protect.

# Lesson 12. AI Is Another Identity

**Listen:** ai-is-another-identity.mp3 (2 min)

**The idea, in three lines**
- AI arrived with the outline that took effect on 1 September 2026, threaded through all five domains, not as a sixth. Attach one AI example to each concept you already know.
- Poisoned training data is an integrity failure. Controls: validate incoming data, restrict access to the training pipeline, log where data came from.
- AI bots and service accounts are identities: same lifecycle, least privilege. Keep an approved list of AI tools and keep sensitive data out of public AI services.

**Impact:** AI questions stop feeling new, because each one is a concept you already know applied to an AI system.

**Who says it:** 4 of the 5 pages on AI in the CC teach this; none disagree on the controls. One page (examcert) dates the AI change to "late 2025"; it came with the 1 September 2026 outline.

**Try it** · Admin · 10 min
On paper or in a phone note, list every AI tool you used this week (chat tools, apps with AI features, browser extensions). For each one:
> **1.** Who makes it?
> **2.** What data did you put into it (work text, personal details, nothing sensitive)?
> **3.** Circle any tool where something private went in. That's a possible leak to a third party.

**Rule to keep**
Every AI tool gets the same rules as any other user.
